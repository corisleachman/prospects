Signal types

Positive signals count too.

Opportunity

A win, award, expansion or momentum creates something worth building on.

Pressure

A client loss, contraction or visible constraint may intensify an existing weakness.

Intent

A new hire, service, system or leader shows the agency is investing in change.

Perspective

The founder has publicly expressed a relevant priority, question or frustration.

Make it earn its place

Why this agency?A verified business signal, not generic admiration.
Why might it matter?A commercial implication framed as a hypothesis.
Why this person?Their role or experience makes their view relevant.
Why respond?A useful, low-effort value exchange.
Do this
  • Verify the signal and record its source.
  • Frame assumptions as questions.
  • Use one commercially relevant detail.
  • Keep the next step small.
  • Edit every AI-produced draft yourself.
Stop if
  • You only have ICP fit and a generic pain point.
  • The compliment doesn't change the conversation.
  • You can't explain why the signal matters.
  • The message implies guaranteed leads or revenue.
  • You're forcing the Growth Audit into the opening.
